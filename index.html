01 — About

Two disciplines, one instinct for finding what's broken.

I spent close to a year working independently as a QA Automation Engineer across early-stage teams — WizCrew Labs, Saltside Technologies, and Cynoteck Technology Solutions — building and maintaining test suites without a QA team around me to lean on. That meant owning the whole quality picture: writing the tests, reading the product like a spec, and flagging the gaps before anyone else saw them.

Before that, I read English at Doon University, where I also served as General Secretary of Elysian, the English Department's literary society — organizing events and holding together a small, opinionated community around language and ideas.

I'm now headed to IIT Jodhpur for an MBA, aiming at tech consulting and product strategy — the kind of work that asks the same question testing does: does this actually hold up, and why or why not.

Testing taught me to read systems the way I was trained to read texts — closely, and for what's not being said.
